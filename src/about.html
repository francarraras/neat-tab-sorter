Neat Tab Sorter is free and open source, made by Fran Carrara. Found a bug or have an idea? Please check the existing issues first, then open a new one.

Credits

Neat Tab Sorter is based on Simple Tab Sorter by Peter White, and was modified from it in 2026 with a rebuilt sorting engine, undo, keyboard shortcuts, natural number order and more. Both are free software under the GNU General Public License v3: you're welcome to redistribute and change them under its terms. This program comes with absolutely no warranty.

Privacy

Neat Tab Sorter doesn't collect or send any data, and it has no ads, analytics or remote code. It reads your tabs' titles and addresses only to sort them, on your computer. Your settings are saved with your browser profile, and each window's undo order is forgotten when you close it or restart your browser.

A note on tab suspenders

Neat Tab Sorter can sort tabs suspended by forks of The Great Suspender, but doesn't recommend any tab suspender. The original The Great Suspender was removed from the Chrome Web Store in 2021 after it changed hands and was flagged as malware, so choose forks carefully, or use Chrome's built-in Memory Saver instead.
